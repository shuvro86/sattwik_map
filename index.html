For Sattwik’s grown-ups

Explore together! Read the clues or turn sound on for your browser’s voice.

A clue appears every 15 seconds, up to five clues. After another 15 seconds to try the last clue, the answer appears. Wrong guesses never lose stars.

The game pauses when this tab is hidden. Stars last for this play session and stay when you change levels. There are 195 countries to discover in every level: 193 UN member states plus Vatican City and Palestine. Every level has four options and a fresh random order. Easy includes flags, Medium uses names, and High uses nearby countries as trickier choices with an optional answer box.

Voice choices depend on this browser and device. Try the speaker button beside a clue to hear one.

Twelve original adventure tunes shuffle during questions. Music gets quieter during spoken clues and when you take a break.

Our maps

Country outlines use public-domain Natural Earth data via datasets/geo-countries. The original twelve country colors are flag-inspired; the expanded collection uses colors by world region. Outlines are simplified educational maps; boundaries follow the source dataset. France’s quiz outline shows metropolitan France and Corsica.

Country facts and flags: mledoze/countries, under the Open Database License. Facts are a bundled snapshot, not live updates. Country coverage follows the 193 UN members plus the two observer states; dependent territories are not separate quiz entries.

Population: World Bank (CC BY 4.0); Vatican City: official 2024 resident count. Photos: Wikimedia Commons, with individual authors, licenses, and source links on each picture. Pictures need an internet connection.
